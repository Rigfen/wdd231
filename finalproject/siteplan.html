Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

Court Trainer

Court Trainer is a basketball training system that combines LED lighting, audio cues, and timing challenges to improve player speed, reaction time, and agility.

Optional Domain: courttrainer.com

Site Purpose

The website explains how the Court Trainer system works, highlights its features, demonstrates training drills, provides setup instructions, and allows visitors to learn about future updates and contact the creator.

Scenarios

  • How does the LED training system improve basketball footwork?
  • What equipment do I need to build and use the Court Trainer?

Color Scheme

  • Dark Blue (#1B365D) – Header, navigation, footer.
  • Orange (#F28C28) – Buttons, accents, and highlights.
  • White (#FFFFFF) – Main background and text areas.

Typography

  • Oswald – Headings
  • Roboto – Body text
